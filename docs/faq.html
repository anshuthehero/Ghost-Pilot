Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about screen invisibility, audio capture, latency, and privacy.

Is Ghost Copilot detectable on Zoom, Meet, or Teams?

No. Ghost Copilot uses operating system window manager flags (WDA_EXCLUDEFROMCAPTURE on Windows and NSWindowSharingNone on macOS). When Zoom or Google Meet captures your screen, the operating system simply skips the Ghost Copilot window buffer. It remains visible only on your physical screen.

Do I have to pay for a Groq API key?

No. Groq offers generous free-tier access that provides high rate limits for both whisper-large-v3 speech-to-text and llama-3.3-70b-versatile inference. A free key easily covers full multi-hour interview sessions.

Can I use a secondary device like a phone or iPad?

Yes! Ghost Copilot includes a built-in mobile web companion. Simply open http://<YOUR_PC_IP>:9471 on your phone or iPad on the same Wi-Fi network. You can prop your phone under your webcam and glance at solutions naturally without keeping any window on your monitor.

What if my interviewer speaks with background noise or an accent?

Ghost Copilot uses OpenAI's Whisper Large V3 hosted on Groq, which is one of the most robust speech-to-text models available for handling accented English, technical jargon, and noisy microphone inputs.

Is my audio or conversation data stored anywhere?

No. The application runs locally on your machine. Audio buffers are held in RAM, sent over TLS to Groq API for transcription, and immediately cleared. No telemetry, audio recordings, or interview logs are retained.
