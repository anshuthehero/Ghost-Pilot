About Ghost Copilot

Building split-second confidence and real-time guidance for software engineers navigating high-stakes technical interviews.

The Motivation

Technical interviews often test trivia recall, niche algorithmic puzzles, and high-pressure syntax memorization rather than real-world problem-solving ability. Even senior engineers can freeze when asked to recite boilerplate code under scrutiny.

Ghost Copilot was built to level the playing field: providing an ultra-fast, contextual safety net that transcribes live dialogue in real time and streams hints, optimal algorithms, and structured talking points directly to your peripheral vision.

Privacy & Local Control

Zero Cloud Logging

Ghost Copilot runs entirely on your local machine. No session recordings, interview transcripts, or personal data are ever uploaded to our servers.

Your Own API Key

All speech and language inference calls go directly from your machine to your personal Groq Cloud account via encrypted HTTPS requests.

Open Source & Community

Ghost Copilot is completely open source under the MIT / Apache 2.0 license. Star us on GitHub, submit issues, and contribute pull requests!
